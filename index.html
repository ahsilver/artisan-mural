How changes work
Getting a good print

How big should my picture be?

Wall murals are seen from a few steps back, so they don't need as many pixels as a printed photo. Use the original file from your camera, not one sent through a chat app, which shrinks it.

  • Use the original file: straight from the camera or phone gallery, not a screenshot.
  • Avoid heavy filters and very dark photos: printed walls look a little darker than screens.
  • Leave room at the edges: 1 in on each side is trimmed when hanging.
  • Not sure? Upload it: the sharpness meter tells you right away, for your exact wall.
